Privacy Policy Effective 20 Nov 2026

What the app accesses

  • user.info.basic: the owner's TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, used only to confirm that videos go to the right account.
  • video.publish: permission to upload and post videos to the owner's account.

What it stores and where

The access token and refresh token issued by TikTok are saved in a private file on the owner's computer, readable only by the owner's user account. A local log keeps the episode number, post ID and publishing time of each video. Nothing is stored on any server run by the app, because the app has no server.

What it does not do

  • It collects no data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services, and can delete the local token file. Data sent to TikTok is handled under TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Contact

Send a direct message to @delegadoonofre on TikTok.

Resumo em português

Privacidade. O app só acessa o perfil básico e a permissão de publicar da própria conta. Os tokens ficam num arquivo privado no computador do dono, sem servidor.
