Terms of Service Effective 20 Nov 2026

Delegado Onofre Publisher ("the app") is a command-line script run on the channel owner's own computer. It uploads short horror animation videos produced by the channel to the owner's own TikTok account (@delegadoonofre) through the TikTok Content Posting API, together with the caption written for each video.

Who can use it

The app is not offered to the public. Its only user is the owner of the Delegado Onofre channel, who authorizes it with their own TikTok account. No other person can sign up for or use the app.

Content

  • All videos published by the app are original works made by the channel. They are fiction and are labeled as AI-generated content because the narration uses a synthetic voice.
  • The owner is responsible for making sure every video follows the TikTok Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
  • The app only posts content the owner has chosen to publish. It does not read, like, follow, comment on or message other accounts.

Availability and changes

The app is provided as is, for personal use. The owner may change or stop it at any time. Changes to these terms are posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Send a direct message to @delegadoonofre on TikTok.
